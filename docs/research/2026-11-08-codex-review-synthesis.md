# Reassessment after Claude's review

November 8, 2026

Claude's later value review improves the hardware argument by moving attention toward an independently enforced industrial command path. The proposed gate has useful implementation progress. Its strongest claims still depend on an observer that accurately represents what the PLC accepted, a usable denial/recovery protocol and isolation that persists after boot. The current code exposes concrete failures at those boundaries. I would prioritize an independent endpoint experiment and a closed-loop comparison before committing the research effort to a general AI firewall.

The best new research hypothesis is **enforcing industrial commands when delivery, acceptance and plant observations are uncertain**. It asks how a gate can preserve a declared invariant and useful operation while its knowledge may be stale or incomplete. The potential contribution would be a bounded implementation and evidence connecting actual network bytes, accepted PLC commands, observation validity and release decisions. Existing work already covers stateful enforcement and partial-observation shielding. The contribution must survive those comparisons.

The short alternative is a host-runtime experiment on RC1 for grammar-restricted vocabulary projection and provably forced token runs. It has an inexpensive feasibility path, although new searches substantially narrow its novelty. The earlier KV-recovery proposal remains a third option if a qualified accelerator-retirement boundary offers a useful improvement.

The detailed audits are [hardware](2026-11-08-codex-review-hardware.md), [applications and experimental design](2026-11-08-codex-review-applications.md), and [gate assurance](2026-11-08-codex-review-assurance.md). [Offline probe results and source hashes](2026-11-08-codex-review-checks.json) record the reproducible findings below.

**What changed in the repo.** The supported baseline is now RC1 on branch `codex/hardware-consolidated-rc1`, with the most recent inspected commit `1969bdd`. It combines v9e BF16 KV and 2,048-token context with sealed ABI2 ownership, a persistent service and qualified reboot startup. The previous v9d/v16 measurements remain historical controls. The review also examined new agent-gate software, frame-level RTL and bridge sources in the working tree. Their source hashes identify the inspected versions, since uncommitted work may continue. **Which parts of Claude's conclusion hold up.** The new focus on physical I/O, authority and predictable enforcement uses the K26 more naturally than a raw token-throughput race. Separating an untrusted proposer from an independently enforced command path is useful. The existing RC1 and typed200 work supply a deployment baseline, arithmetic references, failure-state experience and evidence tooling. The new frozen-rule simulator and OOC RTL are useful development evidence. These assets justify another experiment. They do not yet establish deployment advantage over an external software gate, PLC interlock or MCU with the same policy and information.

**Experimental corrections**

The frozen data do support the printed per-write catch counts. I independently re-counted 37/45 unsafe 0.6B proposals and 394/399 unsafe 4B proposals blocked by `stateful_rules`, with 86/1,034 and 307/2,166 negative-label proposals blocked respectively. However, 924 of those 1,034 negative-label 0.6B writes and 1,594 of 2,166 for 4B simply repeated the current actuator values. An unsafe-write percentage can therefore vary substantially with repetitive commands, even when useful behavior is unchanged. The runs use different episode sample counts and stochastic proposals. Their difference does not establish a general ordering of model danger. [Saved-row arithmetic](2026-11-08-codex-review-checks.json)

The labeler marks a write unsafe only when it introduces a new named hazard during a ten-second constant-action counterfactual. At temperature 91 degrees, raising heater power from zero to 100 is labeled negative because both branches already contain overtemperature. The probe reproduces this exactly. Report physical bounds, onset, duration, severity and recovery separately from incremental attribution of a new hazard type. Plain holds or rejected commands can also leave an already harmful actuator setting in place. [Labeler](../../src/sysone/v2/agent_gate/plant.py:168)

The zero operator false-block count is useful on that scripted operator, but its 1,299 writes are correlated within episodes. Wilson intervals treating writes as independent do not certify a 1% field false-block rate. Human escalation was not simulated. Counting blocked safe proposals as eventual approved successes would require an approval/response budget and measured operator behavior. Pooling hand-designed fault families with LLM proposals is helpful for diagnostic coverage, with the natural task population and weighting still to be defined.

**A research plan that could change the decision**

| Decision stage | Smallest useful result | Condition for proceeding |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Establish endpoint correspondence | Independent TCP/Modbus endpoint logs the accepted command stream while checksum, segmentation, replay, wrong-unit, exception, lost-response and reconnect schedules run through the observer. | Every advertised confirmed state follows from the endpoint history under the declared assumptions. Unknown outcomes remain explicit and recovery is possible. |
| 2. Compare observer strategies | Current optimistic state, reset/hold on uncertainty, fixed expiry, acknowledgment/readback reconciliation and a conservative possible-state observer use identical observations. | The candidate retains more useful operations or recovers faster at equal physical-constraint performance and observation/approval budget. Failure cases remain part of the result. |
| 3. Test actual task utility | Each arm drives its own plant, with paired initial conditions and disturbances. Include ordinary PLC interlocks and an external trusted stateful software gate with the same information. Use independent scenarios or a second plant specification. | A useful result survives comparison with the strong stateful controls, rather than only a range-list baseline. |
| 4. Test the hardware increment | Run the same policy/observer on optimized C and PL/R5. Measure from defined ingress events to actual egress, queue effects, command delivery, recovery and resource cost. Protect configuration for the attacker scope being claimed. | Hardware adds a demonstrable authority or timing property at acceptable useful throughput. Count denial-induced unavailability and receive overload. |

For stage 1, specify the intended operating profile. A restricted transparent bridge may deliberately reset an unusable connection and require reconciliation. A terminating Modbus proxy can report an operation-level rejection while preserving a TCP conversation, with additional implementation and trust dependencies. Support for ordinary TCP segmentation and the meaning of a denied command must be decided before a throughput claim.

For stage 2, the invariant should concern all states consistent with the evidence and assumptions. Confirmed register state and physical state need separate semantics. Sensor bias, elapsed time, autonomous PLC changes and plant drift require declared bounds. Acknowledgments/readbacks should narrow uncertainty without treating a claimed or stale response as fresh evidence. At release, bind approval to normalized command, endpoint, policy generation, relevant state and a single-use request identity. Raw byte equality and a timeout alone do not establish this.

For stage 3, use a controls expert's second specification or an independently maintained simulator/PLC pipeline. Keep the current plant as a regression corpus. Score completed targets, time inside bounds, damage duration, recovery capability, unnecessary holds, extra reads and human burden per episode. Cluster uncertainty by episode rather than by every correlated write. A fail-closed design can be safe for a declared release invariant and still fail the requested task or leave an already dangerous condition unresolved.

The possible contribution is an explicit and tested refinement from evidence and network events to an admissible endpoint action, with a bounded implementation and useful recovery. It remains a hypothesis. If a conventional external software proxy already meets all requirements with the same policy and authority, the hardware increment would be small. An independent counterexample/test artifact or observer study could still be a useful result, with novelty assessed against the existing enforcement literature.

**Two branches worth preserving**

The RC1 grammar experiment can proceed first as an offline measurement. Its head resets the maximum when `token_base=0` and returns a token plus FP32 winning logit. A host can address each allowed row interval through `page`, request local labels starting at zero, map the winner back to the absolute token ID and merge interval winners. Qualify ties, nonfinite behavior, bounds and readback. The current bitstream therefore offers an experimental route without a new head design. Measure legal-row fraction, interval fragmentation and forced-token run lengths. A dense-head-only elimination has an optimistic speed ceiling of 169/125 = 1.352× under Claude's phase estimates. Larger gains require enough adjacent provably unique tokens to use the batch-prefill path. [Detailed hardware audit](2026-11-08-codex-review-hardware.md)

A token-ID jump-forward API alone does not prove equality to masked greedy. XGrammar's documented method greedily tokenizes a forced byte continuation and may differ from the model tokenizer. The exact variant must prove a unique next legal token or qualify an equivalent rule. RC1's observed layer-0 numerical checks and finite full generations also do not prove every layer and mixed B1/B8 schedule byte-identical. That is an explicit experiment. Compare a named scalar constrained reference and current resident A53 implementation before promoting a device advantage.

The application branch with the most independent data is maintenance evidence extraction and acquisition. MaintIE, AssetOpsBench and FailureSensorIQ address different labels and tasks, so keep their original semantics. A sharper study would measure how a compact encoder/generator requests missing evidence and abstains under shift, at matched completion and human effort. ARID, SAIN, Schall's topology-aware validator and existing conformal uncertainty results supply strong controls. This branch can use the existing encoder lineage and retains value even if the decoder optimization or network hardware adds little. [Application review and dataset contracts](2026-11-08-codex-review-applications.md)

**Scope and evidence depth.** This round read Claude's two syntheses, comparison, correction and discussion notes, then the current local implementations and preserved results. It re-counted the two saved decision files, ran three existing tests and executed the three documented Python probes. The literature work targeted the closest mechanisms and numerical claims, using primary venue/publisher text, author papers, standards and public implementation sources. SRIM is abstract-only due to paywall, some venue links were unavailable through browsing, and several recent results are preprints or unreviewed prototypes. Broad first claims and numerical publication-success probabilities are unwarranted from this search. This review adds research notes and evidence only. Hardware timing, endpoint behavior and proposed mitigations require their own qualifications.
